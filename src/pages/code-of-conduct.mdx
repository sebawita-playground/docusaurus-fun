# Code of Conduct

Welcome! Before you dive in, here's the deal. Think of this less as a legal document and more as a vibe check.

---

## The Golden Rule

Treat others the way you'd want to be treated after a good night's sleep and a decent cup of coffee.

That's basically it. Everything below is just elaborating on that one sentence.

---

## Be a Human Being

We're all people here — stressed, caffeinated, and occasionally wrong. That means:

- **Be kind.** Not performatively kind. Just... actually kind.
- **Be patient.** Someone asking a "dumb question" is learning. You were there once too.
- **Be honest.** If something is broken, say so. If you don't know, say that too.

:::tip Good vibes only
Constructive feedback is welcome. "This sucks" is not constructive feedback. "Here's what I think could be better, and why" — that's the stuff.
:::

---

## What We Don't Do Here

Some things just aren't welcome, no matter how strongly you feel about them:

- Harassment, bullying, or being generally terrible to people
- Discrimination of any kind (yes, all kinds)
- Spam, self-promotion overdrive, or treating this space like a billboard
- Gatekeeping — nobody gets points for being the most senior person in the room

:::danger Hard no
If you make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome, you're out. No drama, no lengthy appeal process. Just out.
:::

---

## How to Disagree (Like an Adult)

Disagreements happen. Here's the approved method:

1. Take a breath
2. Assume good intent (until proven otherwise)
3. Engage with the *idea*, not the person
4. If it's going nowhere, walk away — it's okay

:::note Friendly reminder
"I disagree" is fine. "You're an idiot" is not. The difference is surprisingly important.
:::

---

## Give Credit, Take Responsibility

- If someone helps you, say thanks
- If you made a mistake, own it — people respect that way more than a cover-up
- If you use someone's work, credit them

---

## When Things Go Wrong

Nobody's perfect. If you see something that doesn't feel right:

- Reach out to the maintainers
- Be specific about what happened
- We'll take it seriously

We promise not to make reporting feel like filing taxes.

---

## The Short Version

Be cool. Help people. Don't be awful. Have fun.

That's the whole thing.

---

*This Code of Conduct is inspired by common sense and the general principle that people are better when they feel respected.*
